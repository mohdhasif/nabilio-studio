# Torquevale photo credits

Stock photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
(free to use; attribution not required). They illustrate types of workshop work only. They do not show Torquevale staff,
customers, premises or completed jobs, and are captioned as illustrative on the site. Photographer names could not be
retrieved from the build environment, so none are listed rather than guessed.

Photos were checked for business names, number plates, phone numbers and brand logos; candidates showing them were
rejected. `under-bonnet-servicing.jpg` was cropped on the left to remove a partly visible product label. All files were
cropped to 3:2, resized (1,400 px long edge) and recompressed locally (JPEG, quality 78, metadata stripped).
